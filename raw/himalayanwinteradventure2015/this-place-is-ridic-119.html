Up at 7 to hit up the buffet before our dives. The spread was impressive so we stocked up and when I say stocked up I mean packed in some Nutella and fruit and nuts for post dive. 

We went out for our dives before 9 w 4 DMs and 6 people. The boat took us out 40 min to a lovely island where Andy and I did our mask and regulator recovery and then we did a drift dive along the reef. It's been a while since we last dive but it was like no time had past. No wetsuits and I got a little cold in the 85* water. The next dive was closer to our island and consisted of a swim through and then we swam against the current. We saw an octopus, puffer fish, eels, gobis, clown fish, anemones, nudibranch, schools of tropical fish, lion fish, tuna... Loved it. Location Undudhoo, Finolhass. Depth 105, 82. EAN 34%

After a quick dip in the pool we got back to our room and ate the delicious Nutella and bananas and nuts. Mmm. Plus we had a bottle of wine delivered to our room (cheapest way to imbibe). An outside shower is just what we needed to before we walked 30 ft to the beach to relax for the rest of the afternoon. This place is ridiculous. 


I read the rest of the Nitrox book for my test and took a nap then watched the sun set. We hit up the happy hour which included drinks and massages right before dinner of salad and fish. We fell asleep around 9 again. This place is fantastic.
